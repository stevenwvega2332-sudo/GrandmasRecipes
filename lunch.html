Grilled Chicken Wraps

← Back to homepage

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded lettuce
  • 1 diced tomato
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • Ranch dressing

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Grill until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  3. Rest the chicken, then slice into strips.
  4. Fill tortillas with chicken, lettuce, tomato, cheese and dressing.
  5. Fold the sides inward, roll tightly and serve.
