Chicken Alfredo

← Back to homepage

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 12 oz fettuccine
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves minced garlic
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions and drain.
  2. Season chicken and cook in a skillet until it reaches 165°F internally. Slice and set aside.
  3. Melt butter in the skillet and cook garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Add cream and simmer gently. Stir in Parmesan until smooth.
  5. Toss pasta with sauce, top with chicken and serve.
